## Robotics community: experiment planning

Title: A blank worksheet for documenting tactile robotics experiments

We maintain RoboSkin.ai, a tactile robotics research resource. We made a free CSV
to help keep the evidence behind an experiment organized: sensor and dataset
versions, calibration references, time alignment, held-out splits, matched
baselines, independent trials and failures.

The 24 records separate what you planned from what you observed and where the
supporting artifact lives. It is a planning aid, not a validated benchmark or a
safety procedure. No signup is required. If the community permits resource
feedback, we would welcome examples of missing fields that would make it more
useful for reproduction.

https://roboskin.ai/tactile-ai?utm_source=reddit&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=roboskin-resources-20261007#experiment-worksheet
