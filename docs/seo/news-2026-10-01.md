## Article evidence

### 1. TacEx

- Paper: `arXiv:2609.40134v1`, submitted 2026-09-30 16:49:40 UTC.
- Primary sources: [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40134), [full v1 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.40134v1).
- Search intent: understand how tactile uncertainty can direct robot exploration rather than merely enter the policy as another observation.
- Key checks: five seeds for reward-free exploration; ten seeds for diffusion steering; eight LIBERO-90 contact-rich tasks; 500,000 environment steps for most tasks and one million for task 69; 32 × 64 × 3 simulated tactile maps.
- Verification increment: the paper publishes task curves rather than one aggregate eight-task result. The article preserves that boundary and separates tactile input from tactile disagreement in the exploration objective.
- Availability: no official project, code, data, checkpoint or implementation license was linked. The experiments are simulation-only; the article's CC BY 4.0 license is not presented as a software/data license.

### 2. OccluDex

- Paper: `arXiv:2609.39017v1`, submitted 2026-09-30 05:22:28 UTC.
- Primary sources: [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39017), [full v1 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.39017v1).
- Search intent: assess whether 3D vision plus tactile contacts helps a dexterous hand operate when it occludes its own camera view.
- Key checks: 1,690 human sequences and 409,035 synchronized frames; 100 evaluation episodes per setting and three seeds; 99.9/86.6% faucet success and 75.1/83.3% reorientation success for seen/unseen conditions; 17/20 and 14/20 physical successes.
- Verification increment: the abstract's 8.3% and 12.6% claims were recalculated from Table III as 8.3 and 12.55 absolute percentage-point differences after averaging two tasks. They are not described as relative percentage improvements.
- Availability: no project page, code, dataset, model or implementation license was verified. The physical study is 40 trials on one Shadow Hand platform.

### 3. Making Waves membrane-coupled delta array

- Paper: `arXiv:2609.39652v1`, submitted 2026-09-30 12:55:15 UTC.
- Primary sources: [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39652), [full v1 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.39652v1).
- Search intent: understand how a compliant continuous surface changes the object-size limit and control space of a distributed actuator array.
- Key checks: 8 × 8 array, 192 DoF, 43.3 mm pitch, 263 × 325 mm membrane; 392 held-out simulation episodes; 95.1% local-policy success versus 93.5% whole-array success; 38/50 physical successes over five 30–90 mm objects.
- Verification increment: “halves the placement error” is checked as a 6.5-to-3.5 mm median reduction, or 46.2%. The 15 mm cube demo uses scripted primitives; the learned sim-to-real policy covers 30–90 mm.
- Availability: no project, code, CAD, bill of materials, dataset or implementation license was verified. The article's CC BY 4.0 license is kept distinct from those unreleased assets.

### 4. FunCo-Grasp

- Paper: `arXiv:2609.39006v1`, submitted 2026-09-30 05:16:47 UTC.
- Primary sources: [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39006), [full v1 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.39006v1), [official project](https://zblzz.github.io/FunCo-Grasp-Web/), [official repository](https://github.com/zblzz/FunCo-Grasp).
- Search intent: determine how functional correspondence transfers dexterous grasps between robot hands with different morphologies.
- Key checks: 14,011 training grasps over three hands; simulation on three seen and four unseen hands; 92.40% and 74.02% average success; 84/100 ApexHand and 68/100 Revo2 physical successes; 0.293-radian diversity below compared methods.
- Verification increment: UniMorphGrasp's 94.00% seen-hand result is retained as paper-reported and has no four-unseen-hand result. The physical total is verified as 152/200 = 76.00%.
- Availability: the official project exposes figures and videos. The GitHub repository was created September 30 but contained only a 13-byte `README.md` saying “Comming soon”; no code, weights, data or software license was present.
