## Fact-check notes

### GLoTouch

- Institutions: independent researcher, Shanghai Jiao Tong University and the University of Hong Kong.
- Hardware: seven-degree-of-freedom Flexiv arm, six-axis wrist force-torque sensing and an Xense parallel gripper with bilateral visuotactile depth.
- Simulation end-to-end retrieval: 84/100 over five target classes. Real robot: 38/50, with 10 randomized trials per target.
- Global discovery at a 5 m budget is 55% versus 30% for random descent; this is not the same metric as end-to-end retrieval.
- Local-matcher ablation reuses contact-sequence subsets across 60 target/phase samples. The full matcher reaches 54/60 top-1, versus 41/60 for width/action alone.
- Requires a target STL, known container registration and scene cardinality, floor-supported objects and top access. Source code is promised in future tense; no repository or license was linked.

### CoPRE

- Institutions: Duke University, KTH Royal Institute of Technology and Carnegie Mellon University.
- Physical protocol: five resistance conditions, three speeds and three repetitions equals 45 contact trials on each of ARX L5 and Unitree G1.
- Reported recall: 74.1% ARX and 82.2% G1. F90 is 3.5 N and 5.5 N respectively under platform-specific calibration.
- Reference values are separately measured sliding resistances, not force at detection. Model seeds reuse the same physical trials.
- Held-out broad alarm time is 3.32% ARX and 0.86% G1. Calibration sweeps expose sensitivity versus false-alarm trade-offs.
- Official CSV files transcribe rounded manuscript results and index recordings; the page explicitly says they are not raw logs. Downstream placement and insertion are qualitative, without a success rate.

### Berkeley QUAD Hand

- Authors and affiliations: Benjamin Davis and Hannah S. Stuart at UC Berkeley; Chase Kidder at General Motors.
- Four fingers, 11 degrees of freedom, eight degrees of actuation and approximately 1 kg mass.
- Static coverage: 29/33 Feix grasps and 10/11 Kapandji postures. These do not measure autonomous or dynamic task success.
- Minimum backdrive test uses 15 trials per joint. Thumb and index base joints are around 50 gram-force; coupled middle and ring fingers are near 250 gram-force.
- Sustained-load evaluation uses 3 N per fingertip for five minutes and a 6 N dual-ulnar condition. Up to 96 times lower calculated heat reflects measured current plus manufacturer phase resistance under those conditions.
- The printed ulnar linkage yields before actuator stall. No peak-force benchmark, CAD, BOM, controller repository or hardware license is provided.

### LiMA

- Institutions: Peking University and Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence. The manuscript is labeled CoRL 2026, but this report treats it as an arXiv preprint and does not independently assert peer review.
- Hardware: two UR5 arms, two 22-DoF SharpaWave hands, three RealSense D435 cameras, VIVE trackers and MetaGlove Pro gloves.
- Six tasks, 100 demonstrations each and 20 physical trials per method/task. LiMA totals 85/120 successes, or 70.8%; average subtask progress is 78.9%.
- Reported 325 ms is amortized generation latency for a 32-step chunk on one H100, with one Dreamer pass per four Refiner updates. It is not per-action latency.
- Cosmos-Policy is 600 ms under the same batch size and numerical precision; GR00T N1.6 and VPP are faster than LiMA at 270 ms and 225 ms.
- The method is vision-and-proprioception based. Tactile feedback is explicitly future work. The official page provides paper and videos but no code, data or weights.
