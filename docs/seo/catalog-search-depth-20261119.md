## Why these targets

The GelSight Mini (140) and general GelSight sensor (70) estimates overlap in intent and cannot be summed. Its profile addresses integration and reconstruction. The separate comparison guide owns cross-model comparison. The search sample combines manufacturer material and research, and the price query (10) is routed to official terms by the appropriate buying guide rather than used as a headline promise.

DIGIT tactile sensor (30) results also mix the original DIGIT with Digit 360. The parent directory retains both records, and the independent original-DIGIT profile names its Python interface and scope. Broad head terms such as AnySkin (210) and AllSight (170) may describe other entities. The applied phrases add the tactile qualifier. XELA Robotics (140) is broader than XELA uSkin (30); the profile focuses on exact module and hand configuration.

Robotics research services results mixed university research, directories and custom engineering services, while robotics research reports results included trade news and market research. Offer copy narrows each to its actual decision or report scope. Labels and descriptions are reader-facing. Keyword metadata supplements the text and is not a ranking claim.

## Three citable technical additions

- /guides/ros2-tactile-sensing#tactile-timing-audit — distinguish capture skew from sample age and expose how a receive timestamp masks stale data. See official ROS 2 message_filters and QoS documentation. The 7 ms / 39 ms example is a worked trace.
- /guides/lerobot-dataset-format#tactile-split-audit — choose a split unit based on the generalization claim; group clips by original runs and reconcile the manifest against episode offsets. See official LeRobot v3 docs and the pinned reader.
- /guides/tactile-sensor-benchmark-robot-manipulation#paired-touch-trials — record paired outcomes rather than reporting only marginal success rates; separate task success from force and failure costs. TacO provides task and setup evidence. The 20 pairs are illustrative.
