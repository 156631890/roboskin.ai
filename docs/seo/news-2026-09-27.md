## Fact-check notes

### DA-GRD

- MuJoCo tests cover ten rigid objects and 100 shared perturbation episodes per object, with translation up to 5 cm and yaw up to plus or minus 45 degrees.
- The principal policy reaches 84.7% lift success, compared with 63.7% for a fixed 15-scan baseline given the same SE(2) belief. Its 4.13 average probes include zero-probe successes.
- Task-conditioned success is lower: 57.3% in simulation and 38.3% on hardware. The article keeps these separate from physical lift success.
- Hardware uses a UR5, Robotiq 2F-140, two fingertip force-sensitive resistors and a wrist FT300. Six objects receive ten trials each; average lift success is 71.7%.
- No official code, dataset, checkpoint or software license was verified.

### Torque-observation alignment

- The six-degree-of-freedom direct-drive gripper policy runs at 20 Hz and uses joint positions plus consecutive torque differences derived from calibrated motor current.
- The physical ablation evaluates nine in-distribution objects with ten trials per variant. Raw absolute torque reaches 26.7%, while the complete scale-and-difference variants reach 100% under the reported setup.
- The broader test contains 21 objects and 210 trials. The method succeeds in all 90 in-distribution trials and 118 of 120 out-of-distribution trials, or 208 of 210 overall.
- The paper's RTX 5090 and 2,000 training iterations are reported reference conditions, not a proven minimum. Torque differencing suppresses sustained absolute load and assumes slowly varying bias.
- No official implementation, policy, calibration dataset, CAD package or software license was verified.

### Outcome-Sensitive Motion Search

- The study is simulation-only, using a 7-DoF xArm7, 17-DoF ORCA Hand and 50 Hz control. Objects weigh 40 to 60 g and launch at roughly 2.8 to 5.0 m/s.
- Three source sets contain 20,000 rollouts each. Search constructs 99,328 successful demonstrations; final students use three seeds and the same 3,000 held-out conditions.
- The impact-aware teacher scores 85.2% task success and 40.8 N peak force. The final imitation policy reaches 90.3% and 37.3 N, a 5.1-point success gain and 8.6% lower peak force.
- Matched-size ablations separate repair and refinement. The article does not combine their benefits without conditions.
- No physical validation, public implementation, dataset or license was verified.

### Body-Grounded Replanning

- GPT-4o-mini at temperature zero selects among manually defined strategies using joint position, effort, contact, event and history summaries sampled at 20 Hz.
- Simulation uses 100 paired episodes for each of ten conditions. In the asymmetric mobility study, the method averages 96.7% success versus 88.5% for the rule baseline.
- Hardware uses CRANE-X7 with eight trials for each method-condition pair. Body-grounded replanning and environment-only prompting both reach 96.3%; the rule method reaches 66.3%.
- Under restricted mobility, active completion time is 24.57 seconds versus 28.18 for environment-only prompting and 35.40 for rules. Active time excludes language-model pauses, and no latency distribution is reported.
- Button pressing, lid opening and lint-roller use are qualitative demonstrations. No public code, prompt package, logs, dataset or software license was verified.
