## Article evidence

### 1. TacDyn-WAM

- Paper: `arXiv:2610.00638v1`, submitted 2026-09-30 19:40:57 UTC.
- Primary sources: [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00638), [full v1 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00638v1), [official project](https://enyi-bean.github.io/TacDyn-WAM-Page/).
- Search intent: understand why a tactile world model might predict contact dynamics rather than future tactile pixels.
- Key checks: 81.5% across eight UniVTAC tasks and 100 rollouts per task; five physical tasks, 60 demonstrations each and 20 trials per policy per task; 71/100 physical successes without extra pretraining and 85/100 with a 6,000-trajectory OmniViTac subset plus the 300 task demonstrations; 484 ms per 50-action chunk on one A100.
- Verification increment: the article keeps large-scale-pretrained N0 baselines separate, recalculates the hardware aggregates from the five per-task values and preserves the different action-chunk lengths in the latency comparison.
- Availability: official tables and demo video are public; code is labeled coming soon. No training code, weights, real-robot dataset or implementation license was verified.

### 2. TouchTherm

- Paper: `arXiv:2610.01943v1`, submitted 2026-10-01 16:10:08 UTC.
- Primary sources: [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01943), [full v1 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01943v1), [official anonymous project](https://anonymous-research1.github.io/).
- Search intent: determine how an object-level digital twin can support optical tactile and time-varying thermal rendering.
- Key checks: 20 objects; three tactile contacts per object; 300 thermal test windows at each 30- and 45-second horizon; surface MAE 0.465 °C and 0.592 °C; recognition Top-1 20.0% to 34.0% over six seeds; ten-person VR demonstration.
- Verification increment: the 14-point recognition gain is stated as absolute percentage points; real-to-real similarity remains above real-to-sim; ambient relaxation, not surface diffusion, explains most headline cooling accuracy in the ablation.
- Availability: official videos and descriptions are public. The page says code, reconstructed objects and the simulation pipeline will be released upon acceptance; no downloadable assets or implementation license was verified.

### 3. DITTO-X

- Paper: `arXiv:2610.00781v1`, submitted 2026-09-30 22:18:17 UTC.
- Primary sources: [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00781), [full v1 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00781v1), [official project](https://tml.stanford.edu/ditto-x/).
- Search intent: assess whether robot-to-human finger alignment and haptic feedback improve dexterous intervention.
- Key checks: six-person repeated-measures study; regular collection 70.0% versus 46.7%; intervention 78.3% versus 27.7%; 30 policy rollouts per task and evaluation round; two DAgger rounds with 20 interventions each; 1,888 episodes described at 30 Hz over more than 16 hours.
- Verification increment: dataset composition sums exactly to 1,888 (847 + 379 + 662), while the apparent release is not treated as downloadable because Code, Dataset and Hardware Guide are non-links marked coming soon.
- Availability: project videos and episode counts are public; no downloadable dataset, code, hardware guide or release license was verified.

### 4. FlashDexRetarget

- Paper: `arXiv:2610.01849v1`, submitted 2026-10-01 15:15:42 UTC.
- Primary sources: [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01849), [full v1 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01849v1), [official project](https://davian-robotics.github.io/FlashDexRetarget/).
- Search intent: understand whether one reference-conditioned RL policy can amortize dexterous retargeting across many human motions.
- Key checks: 25 single-object and 25 two-object motions; XHand SPIDER success 0.90 in 29 GPU-hours versus CHORD 0.46 in 2,847; Sharpa Wave 0.72 in 33 GPU-hours; 300 million main-benchmark steps and 600 million scaling steps; qualitative hardware replay of three tasks.
- Verification increment: 2,847/29 is 98.2×, supporting but tightening the “up to 100×” headline; SPIDER's cross-object averaging limitation and the stricter ManipTrans-object score are both retained; qualitative replays are not converted into an invented success rate.
- Availability: project videos and tables are public; code is labeled coming soon. No repository, trained policy, trajectory archive or implementation license was verified.
