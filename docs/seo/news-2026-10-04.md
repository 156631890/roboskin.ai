## Article evidence

### 1. ActiveWAM

- Paper: `arXiv:2610.01698v1`, submitted 2026-10-01 13:48:29 UTC.
- Primary sources: [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01698), [full v1 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01698v1), [official project](https://icr-lab.github.io/ActiveWAM/).
- Search intent: determine whether joint camera and arm control improves bimanual manipulation under viewpoint and appearance shifts.
- Key checks: 50 RoboTwin-AV tasks; 100 successful training trajectories per task; 100 evaluation episodes per task, seed and condition across three seeds; 53.3% compound-shift success versus 33.3% Fast-WAM; 30/60 physical full-sequence successes versus 14/60 Fast-WAM; about 165 ms inference on RTX 4090.
- Verification increment: recomputed the 20.0 percentage-point compound difference and 26.7-point physical difference; retained separate policies, prescribed stage switching and excluded human inter-segment actions; distinguished training-time video transformation from raw-history deployment.
- Availability: official project public; ActiveWAM code marked coming soon. RoboTwin-AV materials are linked, but ActiveWAM checkpoints and software license were not verified.

### 2. RADMCS

- Paper: `arXiv:2610.01906v1`, submitted 2026-10-01 15:50:23 UTC.
- Primary sources: [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01906), [full v1 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01906v1).
- Search intent: understand how little underwater thrust is needed for directional haptic guidance and what limits closed-loop use.
- Key checks: eight IRB participants across different subsets; four pool threshold participants split across three configurations; two ocean threshold participants; approximately 10% maximum thrust perceptual threshold; seven wall-following participants; one documented loss of distance at about 12 seconds followed by a stale high-turn command.
- Verification increment: separated the union of eight participants from per-test denominators; preserved the failed P8 distance-estimation run and one-person form-fit finding; did not generalize structured AprilGrid results to natural reef tracking.
- Availability: CC BY 4.0 manuscript. No official code, CAD, dataset or software license verified.

### 3. HumanoidToolBench

- Paper: `arXiv:2610.02089v1`, submitted 2026-10-01 17:21:54 UTC.
- Primary sources: [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02089), [full v1 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.02089v1), [official project](https://snu-pi.github.io/HumanoidToolBench/), [official toolkit](https://github.com/SNU-PI/HumanoidToolBench).
- Search intent: measure where humanoid tool use fails between functional selection, grasping, target contact and completion.
- Key checks: 18 tasks, 55 tool assets and 3,094 demonstrations; 3,003 simulation plus 91 real trajectories; 1,959 L1/L2 attempts yielding 1,200 successful demonstrations; 100 simulation episodes per task; 40 real trials per policy across four cells.
- Verification increment: reconstructed the exact dataset total; kept first-contact as a proxy rather than proof of reasoning; distinguished changes in locomotion and target layout at L2; retained the unrelated-instruction shortcut probe.
- Availability: MIT evaluation code, downloadable ToolBook under CC BY-NC 4.0, published ACT/DP checkpoints; training pipelines not included and third-party terms apply.

### 4. InterEvolve

- Paper: `arXiv:2610.02196v1`, submitted 2026-10-01 17:59:40 UTC.
- Primary sources: [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02196), [full v1 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.02196v1), [official project](https://sirui-xu.github.io/InterEvolve/).
- Search intent: assess whether reward-program search can unlock new contact-rich humanoid tasks without retraining the controller.
- Key checks: 3,866 motor-model training clips; eight simulation task families; 192 tuning plus 192 confirmation rollouts per valid proposal; 86.5% macro success, 2.1 GPU-hours and 0.23 million tokens; off-board GPU for hardware perception, state estimation and policy.
- Verification increment: preserved the 76.8% to 73.6% success dip from round two to three; separated the 86.5% simulation macro-average from qualitative physical demonstrations; disclosed motion capture in the repeated-kick example and the lack of a broad physical success-rate table.
- Availability: project videos public. No official code, data, controller checkpoint or software license verified.
