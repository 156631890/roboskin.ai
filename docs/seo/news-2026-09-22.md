## Fact-check notes

### SpectRobot

- Institutions: Wormsensing and Hugging Face; authors Joseph Rigal, Emmanuel Virot and Caroline Pascal.
- Physical platform: SO-101; ACT in LeRobot 0.5.2; 40 demonstrations per dataset and 80 autonomous trials per condition.
- Vision-only result is 23%; tactile sensor conditions range from 48% to 82%. Overlapping 95% Wilson intervals prevent a resolved sensor ranking.
- Bandwidth-duration configurations are coupled. About 0.3-second histories remain near chance; about 2.3 to 2.9 seconds reach 86% to 92%. This supports temporal context for this task, not a universal 10 kHz ceiling.
- The roughly EUR 100 chain uses a separately collected comparable dataset, so its approximately 90% results are not a matched replacement trial.
- The paper discloses that Wormsensing employs two authors. The linked GitHub organization exposed only an Apache-2.0 project website repository at inspection time; no separate training repository was visible. The Hugging Face profile exposes many datasets and model records, not one curated release.

### Tactile-JEPA

- Institutions: Sber AI, Skoltech, HSE University and AIRI; authors Elizaveta Kovtun, Matvey Konovalov, Andrey Sakhovskiy and Semen Budennyy.
- Evaluates Sparsh-skin, Tactile socks and the DECO-50 Assembly subset. Dataset scale, sensor type, rate and time-window conditions are retained in the article.
- Headline gains are metric-specific: 6.3% lower total force RMSE and 20.8% lower orientation RMSE versus the strongest comparison in those rows.
- It is not uniformly best: MAE obtains 0.4400 policy action RMSE on DECO-50 versus 0.4634 for Tactile-JEPA.
- The repository contains JEPA code and configs, but its README focuses on earlier Sparsh work, example configs include author-local paths, and no paper-specific checkpoint was identified. The inspected license is CC BY-NC 4.0.

### Touch2Robot

- Institutions: ShanghaiTech, BIGAI, Shanghai Jiao Tong University and Beihang University.
- Hardware: xArm6, LEAP Hand, TwinTac and binary FSR sensors; MANUS and Juqiao gloves; Meta Quest 3; FoundationPose.
- Four-task replay average is 72.1% versus 37.9% for visual feedback. The metric is staged completion, not uniformly binary success.
- Downstream Diffusion Policy average is 62.0% versus 32.9%; per-task variance is large in some cells.
- Physical contact agreement is 44.19% F1 with 6.38% false-positive rate. This limits claims about contact fidelity.
- User study has ten participants on one rotation task. Cross-hand XHand and Shadow Hand results are qualitative VR outputs. The project page showed media and an appendix, but no public code, dataset or license.

### When Does Touch Matter?

- Institutions: University of Southern California and University of New Mexico; authors Hao Jiang, Luis Dominguez and Daniel Seita.
- Hardware: xArm7, LEAP Hand, four PaXini pads with 508 three-axis taxels, two RealSense cameras; 320 demonstrations.
- Controlled study: eight variants by five conditions by five trials equals 200 physical trials. The main matched comparison is 24/25 combined versus 14/25 vision-only.
- Vision-only still executes through the same wrench-based compliant controller. The policy observation changes; physical compliance does not.
- External ClutterDexGrasp zero-shot deployment did not share the controller and required an emergency stop, so it is not included as a clean matched physical baseline.
- Project page exposed explanations and videos but no code, policy or dataset download at inspection time.
