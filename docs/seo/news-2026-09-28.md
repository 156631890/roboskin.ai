## Fact-check notes

### Blind Grasp Reflex

- The 20-degree-of-freedom Robotis HX5-D20-MRT hand policy sees a five-frame history of joint position and position-command error. It runs at 11.9 Hz over a 1 kHz low-level loop.
- On 78 YCB and 3,028 GraspXL objects, the modular policy reports 96% and 95% simulated grasp success, compared with 98% and 97% for the end-to-end reinforcement-learning baseline.
- Dynamic-object and object-pose generalization tests report 92% and 90% for Blind Grasp Reflex, compared with 8% and 15% for the end-to-end baseline. These are simulation results.
- Hardware examples cover 29 objects, but the paper does not provide repeated trials or a physical success rate. The article does not turn qualitative examples into a percentage.
- The project page labels code “Coming Soon.” The CC BY 4.0 manuscript license does not license unreleased code, weights or environments.

### TACTIC

- TACTIC crosses five encoders and five fusion mechanisms across board wiping, vase wiping, USB-C insertion and screw insertion. Main ablations use 20 rollouts per condition; the reported evaluation total is 2,180 rollouts.
- USB-C termination reaches 80% for ResNet18 plus concatenation versus 55% for vision-only. Screw termination reaches 90% for Sparsh-CLIP-T and T3-CLIP-T under the reported protocol.
- The 2,180 evaluation rollouts are not the training-data count. The official Hugging Face collection contains four datasets with 104, 102, 101 and 103 episodes, or 410 total.
- Dataset cards display MIT licenses and files are downloadable, but several README bodies are empty. The project still labels policy code “coming soon.”
- The article preserves the paper's central conditional result: no encoder-conditioning combination dominates across all four tasks.

### VisTacAlign

- The policy has 23.5 million parameters, predicts 64 action steps, executes 32 and replans. Reported inference is 269 ms on an RTX 4080.
- Drill pickup rises from 50% with 26 robot demonstrations to 70% when 26 aligned human demonstrations are added. In the low-robot-data study, aligned touch reaches 55% versus 35% robot-only and 30% with touchless human data.
- Lego insertion reaches 80% with 50 human plus 30 robot demonstrations, compared with 10% from 30 robot demonstrations alone, over 20 trials.
- Strawberry results are reported over ten trials per size. Damage is not quantified, so the article describes task completion rather than gentle-handling success.
- The task-specific mapping reduces each fingertip to one normal-force value, discarding spatial pressure and shear. The project page labels code and paper “coming soon,” though the arXiv paper is available; no public code or dataset was verified.

### PHASE

- PHASE uses 122 prior demonstrations plus four target demonstrations per shape on a UR5e with a compliant wrist and 3-by-3 tactile sensor.
- Five peg shapes receive 20 trials each, producing 100 trials per method. Circle and square provide prior experience; rectangle, oval and hexagon are unseen target shapes.
- Normal-start success is 77% for PHASE versus 64% for the strongest baselines, a 13-percentage-point gap. Shifted-start success is 47% versus 17%, a 30-point gap.
- The method segments demonstrations after a contact peak when estimated-torque variability stabilizes, then uses FastDTW to retrieve matching phases for an ACT policy.
- The official project supplies paper, poster and video links but no public code, policy, dataset or software license. OMRON SINIC X confirms IROS 2026 acceptance but is not independent validation.
